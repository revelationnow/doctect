# References & referrer formulas

*Episode 8 · Advanced · 03:31*

## Welcome

Some pages belong in two places. March the fourteenth belongs to March, and it also belongs to Week 11. This episode is about references: how one page lives in both, and how to link and label across them.

## One page, two places

In the hierarchy, open Weeks, then Week 11. Its seven rows are in italics, with a small link icon. They aren't pages. They're references: pointers to the real days, which live under March.

Select one, and instead of a title and fields there is just a card: this node links to March 14. A reference has nothing of its own. Anything that lands on it goes straight through to the real day.

Why not simply copy the days? Because a copy is a second page. Write on one, and the other never knows. A reference keeps one page, reachable from two places, and the PDF prints it only once.

## Making references

Let's make some of our own. On the top page, Add New Page, and call it Birthdays. It should list its pages the way Weeks and Notes do, so it gets the List Index template. A new page borrows the first template in the project, here Year View, so this step matters.

Hover Birthdays, and the link button, Link Existing Page, opens a picker: the same tree as the sidebar. I'll unfold Quarter 1, and then March. Every day of March is in here, each one a real page. I want March the fourteenth, and clicking it makes the reference.

One more, from the summer. Quarter 3, July, and July the second. Each pick is added at the end of the list, so add them in the order you want them to appear. And there's no limit: a page can be referenced from as many places as you like.

Birthdays now lists both days, and each one is a link to the real day page. In the sidebar, the new rows are italic: references, not copies.

To reach Birthdays from the year page, one change: its second list shows three sections, starting at four. Make that four, and Birthdays joins Weeks, Notes and To-Do Lists.

## Getting back

From a day, the way back to its week is a link called Go to Referrer. On the Day View template, the weekday chip in the corner uses it: find the reference that points at this day, and go to the page it sits on.

March 14 now has two references, the week's and the birthday list's. Go to Referrer takes the first one ever made, which is the week's, so this chip still means: my week.

## Labels from references

Month View's week numbers come from a formula instead, called child referrer. It asks which page references this month's days, and prints that page's title.

It has four parts: which day to start from, how many to scan, which kind of page to prefer, and which field to print. You rarely type it by hand. Insert Referrer Field opens a builder that writes it for you.

## Following the chain

Let's export and follow it all the way. It's a long document, more than thirteen hundred pages, so the export takes a moment. It walks every page in order, skips the references so each day prints only once, and writes every link as a real PDF link.

From the year page, Birthdays, which lists both days. Then March 14, the real day page. And the weekday chip, Sat, goes to Week 11, the page that holds its reference.
