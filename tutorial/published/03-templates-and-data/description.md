Pages, templates & data binding | PDF Architect tutorial 3 of 12 (Foundations)

The recipe-book cover from Episode 2 becomes a notebook. We add pages to the hierarchy, give them a template of their own, and use placeholders and data fields so every page fills in its own name, servings and cooking time, plus a field every page inherits from the cover.

In this video:
• How pages (nodes) and templates fit together
• Adding, renaming and duplicating pages
• Creating a template, sizing it, and copying elements between templates
• Placeholders like {{title}}, and the Preview selector
• Data fields, Insert Data Field, and why a missing field comes out blank
• Fields inherited from parent pages, and overriding them on one page
