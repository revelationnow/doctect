Your first planner, start to finish | PDF Architect tutorial 1 of 12 (Foundations)

Start here. We create a complete 2026 planner from a built-in preset: more than thirteen hundred pages, where every date links to its own page. Along the way you learn the three columns of the editor, how pages (nodes) relate to page designs (templates), and how a single export turns it all into a PDF with working links. No account needed.

In this video:
• Creating a project from a preset
• The editor's three columns: pages, canvas, properties
• Drilling from a year down to a single day
• Why ten templates are enough for 1,368 pages
• Previewing a template against different pages
• Exporting a PDF and checking that its links work
