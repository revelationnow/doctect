# Keeping your work safe

*Episode 15 · In depth · 02:59*

## Welcome

Everything you make here lives in your browser, and it saves as you work. This episode is about what that means, the one place it can bite, and how to keep a copy somewhere safer.

## Tabs

Every open project is a tab: here's the recipe book, and here's the 2026 planner. Each one keeps its own page, zoom and undo history while you're away.

The plus opens New Project, from episode one. Each tab shows its project's name, and renaming the top page in the tree renames the tab.

## Saved locally

Watch the badge beside the tabs while I rename this to Family Cookbook. Saving locally while I type, then Saved locally.

There's nothing to press. Every change is written into this browser's own storage a moment later. Reload, and both tabs come straight back.

Locally is the word to notice. It means this browser, on this computer. Clear the site's data, or switch computers, and these tabs won't be there. Nor will any presets you've saved.

## Closing a tab

Closing a tab is where work gets lost, so the app asks first. Hover a tab, and its cross appears.

As it says, closing removes the whole project from this browser, and Close without Saving keeps no copy. This planner came straight from a preset, so here that's fine.

Anything you've worked on deserves Save JSON & Close. It downloads the project as one file, then closes the tab.

Closing the last tab leaves a blank project behind. And here's the file: plain text, holding every page, template and variant. Keep it somewhere that isn't this browser.

## Getting it back

The app has no Open button for these files. Instead, copy the whole file, and paste it into the JSON editor, in a fresh tab.

In Text mode, select all, paste, and Apply Changes. The recipe book is back, and the tab reads Family Cookbook again.

Apply replaces whatever the tab held, so use a blank one. Paste into the wrong tab, and one Ctrl+Z takes it back.

## A copy in the cloud

The other copy outside your browser is a cloud save, from episode eleven. I'm signed in, so Cloud offers Save to cloud (new): a version on the server, with a message.

Closing a tab never deletes those versions. The cookbook hasn't changed since I saved it, so this time Close without Saving loses nothing.

My projects, in the account menu, lists everything you've saved to the cloud. Here's the cookbook: private, with one version.

Open loads its latest version into a new tab, in any browser where you sign in. And here it is, back in the editor.

It's still linked to its cloud project: Cloud offers Save to cloud, not new, and Version history. The next save adds to the same history.

So: let it save as you work, close tabs with Save JSON & Close, and cloud-save the milestones. See you in the next one.
