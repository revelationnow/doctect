Grids from the ground up | PDF Architect tutorial 7 of 19 (Building blocks)

Every calendar in a planner is a grid. On a small Q4 2026 project with an empty Month template, we build a month calendar one grid setting at a time: the source, columns, cell size and gaps, what each cell says, a static and then a dynamic offset, a window of the list, and traversal from the cover down to its days. Then the links, in the exported PDF.

In this video:
• One cell per child, and what the Source decides
• Cols and gaps, and why W and H are one cell
• Display Template and the field buttons
• Static and dynamic offsets, and the Sunday wrap
• Final Data Subset: a window of the list
• Deep Traversal: from a cover down to its days
