Real calendars: offsets, slicing & traversal | PDF Architect tutorial 7 of 12 (Advanced)

Calendars are grids with one extra problem: day 1 has to land under the right weekday. Using the 2026 Planner, we look at the dynamic offset that solves it, slices that turn one list into separate windows, and then build a mini calendar on the year page by reaching three levels down the tree.

In this video:
• Why calendars need an offset, and Static versus Dynamic
• How weekday_num and the adjustment put day 1 in the right column
• The wrap-around for months that start on a Sunday
• Final Data Subset: one source, separate windows
• Deep Traversal: grids over grandchildren
• Why an empty grid usually means an empty source
