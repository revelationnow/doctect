Every kind of link | PDF Architect tutorial 13 of 19 (In depth)

Episodes 5 and 8 used three of the eight link targets. This one covers the other five on the 2026 Planner: a fixed page, a child by position, an ancestor by level, a child's referrer and a web address. Along the way: what Go to Sibling does at the end of a list, which pages drop a link, what deleting a reference or a page does to the links around it, and a click through every one of them in the exported PDF.

In this video:
• Go to Specific Page for home links and jumps across the tree
• Go to Child (by Index) stores a position, not a page
• Go to Sibling past the end of a list, and where it stops
• Go to Ancestor (Level) counts from the page that draws it
• Go to Child's Referrer: Start Index, Count / Direction and the filter
• Open URL, and what deleting does to links and references
