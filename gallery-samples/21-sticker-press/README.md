# The Sticker Press

A 500-sticker book for reMarkable and Boox devices, sorted and cross-referenced like a real
sticker album. Every sticker is sourced, not drawn for this product — see **Sourcing &
Licensing** below before anything else. 17 category sheets, six Lucide colourways, two sizes
per sticker, an A–Z index, a keyword index, and a Credits page that carries the full attribution
text, generated across four device variants that share one page order.

## Why you'll like it

- **Real stickers, real credit.** All 500 come from two open icon sets — Lucide and Twemoji —
  named plainly, with both licences quoted in full on page 3 of every variant. See **Sourcing &
  Licensing** for the exact split and the licence text.
- **Sorted the way you'd actually look for one.** 17 categories from Tabs & Flags to Symbols &
  Miscellaneous, each with its own home sheet, plus two ways to search: an A–Z index by name and
  a keyword index by theme (up to 6 matching names per keyword before "+N more").
- **Six colourways, one tap away.** Lucide stickers ship in Outline, Amber, Green, Blue, Red, and
  Ink; a switcher band along the bottom of every Lucide sheet jumps to the same page position in
  a different colourway. Twemoji is never recoloured — its sheets say so and print full colour
  throughout.
- **Two sizes on every sheet.** Each sticker prints at a large and a small size side by side
  (48pt/24pt on Paper Pro, scaled proportionally on the other three devices), so a small
  bullet-tab sticker and a big illustration sit at the same visual density.
- **Four devices, one book.** Paper Pro, Paper Pro Move, Boox Note Air 5C, and Paper Pure all
  generate the identical 190-page structure in the identical order — only each device's own
  physical layout differs. The narrower Move gets an 8-chip reduced category rail instead of all
  17, so labels stay legible on its smaller page.
- **Never more than two taps from Credits.** Every sheet's footer carries a Credits link back to
  page 3, so the attribution is reachable from anywhere in the book, not just the front matter.

## Workflow

1. Open **Start Here** for the map of the book, or jump straight to the A–Z or keyword index if
   you already know what you're after.
2. Flip to **Colour Guide** to see the six colourways side by side.
3. Tap a category chip along the top of any sheet to jump to that category's home sheet; tap a
   colourway swatch along the bottom to see the same stickers in a different colour.
4. Lasso a sticker off any sheet and paste it onto a note or planner page elsewhere in your
   document — the first colourway sheet in each category prints the sticker's name; later
   colourway sheets are colour-only browsing, reachable from that labelled sheet via the
   switcher.
5. Use **Your Workspace** (blank, dot-grid, and ruled variants) as a place to try layouts, or
   flip **Example: A Filled Page** / **Example: Annotated** to see one worked mockup first.

## Configuration

None. Unlike this gallery's other flagships, The Sticker Press has no `DEFAULT_CONFIG` — the
500-sticker set, 17 categories, and six colourways are the product, not a count to dial. Every
generation produces the same 190-page book.

- 1 cover, 1 Start Here, **1 Credits & Sources (page 3)**, 1 Contents, 1 Colour Guide

## Navigation

- Cover (full-page tap and CTA) → `start_here`
- Start Here's seven chips → Contents, Colour Guide, the worked example, Your Workspace, the
  first A–Z index page, the first keyword index page, and Credits & Sources
- Category rail across the top of every sticker sheet (17 chips on Paper Pro/Note Air 5C/Paper
  Pure, 8 on the narrower Move — the current category always shown and bolded) → that category's
  home sheet
- Colourway switcher across the bottom of every Lucide sheet (six swatches, current one bolded,
  jumping to the same page position in another colourway) plus a Credits chip; Twemoji sheets show
  a "Full colour — not recoloured" note and a Credits chip instead
- A–Z index rows and keyword index rows → each sticker's home sheet (its first-colourway or
  Twemoji sheet)
- Contents lists every section by full name, including all 17 categories, each linking to that
  category's home sheet
- The worked example shows **EXAMPLE** chrome with a working skip link to the blank workspace;
  the blank workspace's dot-grid and ruled variants sit alongside the plain one

## Sourcing & Licensing

**None of the artwork in this book is original.** Every one of the 500 stickers is drawn from one
of two open icon sets, and this product's actual work is the curation, the six-colourway system,
the two lookups, and the four-device layout engine around them — not the pictures themselves.

- **Lucide** (lucide.dev) — 330 stickers. Licensed **ISC** (288 of the 330); copyright for Lucide
  itself is held by Lucide Contributors 2025, with portions held by Cole Bemis 2013-2023 as part
  of Feather. The remaining 42 are Lucide icons derived from the Feather icon set and carry
  Feather's original **MIT License**, Copyright (c) 2013-2023 Cole Bemis. Lucide stickers appear
  in six colourways (Outline, Amber, Green, Blue, Red, Ink) at two sizes each.
- **Twemoji** (github.com/jdecked/twemoji) — 170 stickers, graphics licensed **CC-BY 4.0**
  (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International) by Twitter, Inc and other contributors, as a
  collective work with no individual artist credited per graphic. Twemoji is never recoloured — it
  prints in full colour throughout, at the same two sizes as Lucide.

Full licence text for both sets, with the per-source counts read live off the generated book (so
they can't drift from a real run), is on the **Credits & Sources** page — page 3 of every variant,
and one tap away from every sheet's footer via its Credits chip. Upstream snapshots recorded in
`vendor/manifest.json`: Lucide `main@23f9abc`, Twemoji `main@b6b55fe`.
