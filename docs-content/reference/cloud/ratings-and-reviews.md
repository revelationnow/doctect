The **Reviews** section at the bottom of every project page takes a **1–5 star rating** (required) plus optional text (up to 2,000 characters). There's exactly **one review per person per project** — come back and the form reappears pre-filled under **Your review**, and saving replaces (upserts) what you wrote before, so there's no pile-up of duplicates. Writing needs an account with a [username](/docs/reference/username); reading is free, signed out included; and **you can't review your own project** — the server refuses it outright, so there's no way to nudge your own average.

The project's **average** can't drift — it's recalculated from the reviews whenever one is saved, changed, or deleted — and is shown rounded to one decimal as partially-filled stars on the title, the Reviews heading, and the gallery card. The gallery's **Top rated** sort weighs it by how many reviews stand behind it, so one five-star review can't outrank a hundred averaging 4.8; the **4+ stars** filter uses the plain average. You can **delete** your own review any time — and, tellingly, deleting *isn't* username-gated even though writing is, so an account that never picked a handle can still clean up after itself. A flag icon on each review reports it to the moderators (also available signed out); reporting is input to moderation, not a public downvote.

See [Rating a project](/docs/gallery/ratings-reviews-profiles#rating-a-project).
